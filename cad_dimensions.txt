================================================================================
              RUBIK'S SOLVER - PART DIMENSIONS FOR CAD / 3D PRINTING
================================================================================

All dimensions are in millimetres (mm) unless stated otherwise.

How much to trust each number:
  [DRAWING]  Taken from the manufacturer's dimensioned drawing. Design to it.
  [SPEC]     From the manufacturer's or seller's spec sheet, no drawing.
  [LISTING]  From the product title or a retailer listing. Check with calipers.
  [MEASURE]  Not found online, or varies between sellers. Measure your part
             before you design around it.

Print tolerances: an FDM printer usually prints holes slightly SMALL and
outside dimensions slightly LARGE. The "print" suggestions below add a typical
clearance (+0.2 to +0.4 mm). Print a small test piece first and adjust for
your own printer.

1. STEPPER MOTOR (x6) - NEMA 17, STEPPERONLINE 17HE15-1504S
================================================================================

The Amazon listing ("1.5A 42Ncm 42x42x38mm 1.8deg 4 Wires with 1m Cable and
Connector") matches STEPPERONLINE model 17HE15-1504S. All numbers below are
from that model's dimensioned drawing [DRAWING].

  FRONT VIEW (looking at the shaft)          SIDE VIEW

     |<------ 42.3 max ------>|                  |<-23.5->|<--- 38 --->|
     |    |<--- 31 --->|      |                  |        |            |
     +------------------------+               ___|________|____________|
     | o                   o  |  ---           |  shaft  ||  body       |
     |       .------.         |   ^            |  O5     ||             |
     |      /  O22   \        |   31           |=========||             |
     |      \  boss  /        |   v            |  D-cut  ||             |
     |       '------'         |  ---           |___20____||_____________|
     | o                   o  |                         ^^
     +----------[conn]--------+                    boss 2 tall

  Body / flange
    Flange (square)                 42.3 max  (nominal 42)
    Body length (flange to back)    38 (reference)
    Corner chamfers                 yes, standard NEMA 17

  Mounting
    Hole pattern                    31 +/- 0.2, square (centre to centre)
    Thread                          M3, 4 holes in the front face
    Thread depth                    4.0 min
      -> Choose screw length = plate thickness + 3.5 (no more than ~4 of
         thread engaged, so the screw doesn't bottom out).
      -> Print: clearance holes 3.4 dia.

  Front boss (pilot) - the raised ring around the shaft
    Diameter                        22 (+0 / -0.05)
    Height above flange             2 +/- 0.25
      -> Print: pocket 22.4 dia x 2.3 deep to centre the motor, or a
         through-hole of at least 22.5 dia.

  Shaft (single shaft, front only)
    Diameter                        5 (+0 / -0.012)
    Length from flange face         23.5 +/- 0.5
    D-cut (flat) length             20 +/- 0.2, measured back from the tip
    Across the flat                 4.5 +/- 0.1  (the flat is 0.5 deep)
      -> Print: bore 5.2 dia with a flat at 4.6, so the coupler slides on
         and can't spin on the shaft.

  Connector and cable
    Motor-side connector            JST PH 2.0 mm, 6-pin (PH-6AWJ socket)
    Connector position              Bottom edge of the motor, sticks out
                                    about 6.5 below the flange outline and
                                    sits about 10 from the back of the body.
                                    Leave room in the frame for this plug!
    Connector width                 about 16 (pin span)
    Cable length                    1000 +/- 15
    Wires                           4 x AWG26 (UL1007)
    Far end of the cable            4-pin 2.54 mm female housing
                                    (plugs onto a 2.54 mm pin header)
    Wire colours                    BLK = A+, BLU = A-, GRN = B+, RED = B-
                                    (BLK/BLU = coil A, GRN/RED = coil B)

  Other
    Weight                          about 280 g each (about 1.7 kg for six)
    Holding torque                  0.42 N*m
    Rated current                   1.5 A per phase
    Step angle                      1.8 deg (200 steps per revolution)

3. MOTOR LAYOUT AROUND THE CUBE
================================================================================

  One motor sits on each of the six faces, shaft pointing at the centre of
  the cube (motor n turns face n, in the order U R F D L B, see src/motion.c).

  Useful numbers:
    Half the cube                   27.75   (cube centre to a face)
    Half a motor flange             21.15   (42.3 / 2)

  The motors cannot hit each other: each one is 42.3 wide, which is smaller
  than the 55.5 cube face it sits on. So all six fit around the cube with
  plenty of room, as long as each flange is beyond its cube face.

  Distance from cube centre to each motor flange face:
      = 27.75 (to the cube face)
      + gap between the cube face and the coupler (suggest 1-2)
      + coupler thickness in front of the cube
      ... and the shaft (23.5 long) must reach into the coupler.
  Pick the coupler design first, then this distance.

  The cube must be able to come out: plan for at least one motor (usually
  the top one) to be removable or on a hinge/slide.

4. STEPPER DRIVER (x6, 2 spare) - BIGTREETECH TMC2209 V1.3
================================================================================

  Board size                        20.32 x 15.24  (0.8 x 0.6 in)  [SPEC]
  Form factor                       "StepStick": two rows of 8 pins
  Pin pitch                         2.54
  Row spacing                       12.7 (0.5 in), standard StepStick
                                    [MEASURE to confirm]
  Height with pins and heatsink     [MEASURE] - leave at least 15 above the
                                    board for the heatsink and air flow.
  Pin order (from BTT wiki)
    Control side (J1)               EN, MS1, MS2, PDN_UART, PDN_UART, CLK,
                                    STEP, DIR
    Power side (J2)                 VM, GND, A2, A1, B1, B2, VCC_IO, GND

  Electrical numbers that affect the design
    Motor supply (VM)               4.75-28 V (we use 12 V)
    Current                         2 A RMS / 2.8 A peak max
    Current setting                 I_RMS = Vref / 1.41
                                    (Vref about 1.4 V gives about 1 A)
    Cooling                         The drivers get warm. Put them where air
                                    can move, not in a sealed box.

  The Vref potentiometer is adjusted from the top with a small screwdriver,
  so keep the top of every driver reachable after assembly.

6. TFT DISPLAY - 1.8" ST7735, 128 x 160 (lab-provided)
================================================================================

  The exact module was provided by the lab and its brand isn't known, so
  these are typical values for this display size only [MEASURE]:

    Board (typical)                 about 34 x 56 to 58
    Mounting holes (typical)        4 corner holes, about 2-3 dia
    Visible screen area (typical)   about 28 x 35
    Screen orientation              Portrait 128 x 160 pixels in the code

  Measure: board size, hole positions, where the visible area sits on the
  board (it is usually off-centre, toward the end without the pin header),
  board thickness with the glass, and the header position.

7. IR RECEIVER (lab-provided)
================================================================================

  [MEASURE] Bare 3-pin receivers (VS1838B type) and small breakout boards
  are both common. Measure the body, lens position and pin spacing
  (usually 2.54).
  The lens must SEE the remote: leave a window or hole in the case in
  front of it.

14. MEASURE-BEFORE-YOU-DESIGN CHECKLIST
================================================================================

  [ ] Cube centre cap: size, depth, and gap to neighbouring pieces
  [ ] TFT: board outline, hole positions, visible-area position, thickness
  [ ] IR receiver: body size, lens position
  [ ] TMC2209: height with heatsink, header row spacing
  [ ] Pico W: height with headers soldered
  [ ] Proto board actually used: outline and corner holes
  [ ] Capacitor lead spacing
  [ ] Screw terminal and barrel jack bodies
  [ ] One motor: confirm 31 mm hole pattern and 22 mm boss with calipers
  [ ] Print test: a 5 mm D-bore and a 22 mm boss pocket, to set your
      printer's clearances before printing the full frame
